# Content Requirements

This document specifies content needed to build future experiences. It does not repeat the inventory/findings in `CONTENT_AUDIT.md`.

## Publication contract

Every technical/commercial field needs `source`, `verificationStatus` and `verifiedAt`. Public facts must be `verified`; preview can display `CLIENT VALIDATION REQUIRED`. Every asset needs owner/rights, model/variant association, alt and focal point. Missing content collapses cleanly; no placeholder copy, fake review, stock or specification.

## Cross-site

- Approved naming/tone glossary: Motos, ATV, Side-by-Side, families, Uruguay terminology.
- Logo variants, brand book, typography rights and cyan usage guidance.
- Contact/legal identity, privacy copy, consent version, social URLs.
- Navigation labels/descriptions and footer data.
- Default OpenGraph asset and metadata rules.
- CTA labels, response-time claims and commercial disclaimers approved.

## Home

- Current campaign proposition, date/owner and one primary CTA.
- Representative hero media with mobile/desktop crops and rights.
- Short value propositions for Motos, ATV and Side-by-Side.
- One featured product/family with reason and availability.
- Racing/Adventure/Technology modules with verified proof and related products.
- Community asset/story only if available.
- Local network/backing proof, contact and claims with source/date.
- Fallback editorial ordering when campaign/module expires.

## Listings and family/use landings

- H1/intro, family/use definitions and who each path serves.
- Product descriptors, correct cutout/action image and 1–2 verified decision facts.
- Controlled filter values and display labels.
- Editorial default order and featured rationale.
- Empty-state guidance and category-specific FAQs only if sourced.
- SEO title/description/canonical and unique landing copy when indexable.

## Product card

- Model name, family/use descriptor, canonical link.
- Correct image/alt/focal point.
- Two category-relevant verified facts maximum.
- Price summary/status when authorized.
- Compare eligibility and commercial availability.

## Product detail

- Identity: official local name, family, variant/year and positioning sentence.
- Hero media with correct model/color and responsive crops.
- Availability and price object with currency, validity/source/disclaimer.
- 3–5 verified key facts.
- Benefits and modular copy for Design/Performance/Technology/Capabilities as relevant.
- Feature proof: description, media, applicable model/variant and source.
- Colors/variants and local availability caveat.
- Gallery/video/documentation with rights, captions and transcripts where needed.
- Complete normalized specs, weight type and units.
- Test ride/quote/WhatsApp/dealer eligibility.
- Related models with editorial reason; related experience/articles.
- Warranty/service/support facts approved for Uruguay.
- SEO/OG/Product structured-data inputs; do not supply rating/stock unless real.

### Demo validation flags

- 450SR, 800MT Touring, 700CL-X Heritage and ZFORCE 1000 Sport require corrections/validation listed in `CONTENT_AUDIT.md`.
- CFORCE 450L and UFORCE 800: architecture/media slots may be designed, but specs/claims remain **CLIENT VALIDATION REQUIRED**.

## Compare

- Stable vehicle IDs and compare group.
- Normalized attribute dictionary per category: key, label, group, unit, sort order and explanation.
- Verified values with explicit `Not informed` state.
- Category-specific decisive attributes and CTA eligibility.
- No editorial winner; optional guidance explains how to interpret an attribute.

## Dealer/Service locators

- Verified Location NAP: name, address, department, locality, coordinates.
- Opening hours and exceptions.
- Phones, WhatsApp, email, website and preferred contact.
- Capabilities (`sales`, `service`, `parts`, `testRide`) and categories served.
- Directions URL and geocoding verification.
- Temporary notices/status and verification owner/date.
- Service-specific appointment/warranty/parts instructions.
- General fallback contact when no result exists.

## Location detail

- All locator fields plus useful unique description, entrance/accessibility notes, capability details and approved image/logo.
- Sufficient local content for indexation; otherwise no standalone page.
- No stock, reviews, awards or service claims without verified source.

## Quote

- Product/category options and commercial statuses.
- Uruguay department list; verified sales locations for optional dealer.
- Privacy notice, consent label/version and channel explanations.
- Approved success copy, SLA if real, routing owner and fallback contact.
- Error/retry/antispam copy.

## Test ride

- Eligible products and eligible locations/capabilities.
- Clear “request, not booking” explanation.
- Approved coordination process, success expectations and fallback when unavailable.
- Privacy/consent and contact preference copy.

## Contact

- Topics and routing ownership.
- Corporate contact, hours and escalation/fallback.
- Privacy/consent, success and error copy.

## Experience hub

- One differentiated proposition per Racing, Adventure, Technology, Community.
- Hero/story assets with rights and localized context.
- Proof points with source/date and explicit related products/families.
- CTA per vertical and content freshness owner.

### Racing

Verified teams/results/credentials, dates, rights and explanation of relationship to SR/product. Never imply race technology transfer without approved proof.

### Adventure

Touring/exploration themes, verified MT capabilities, local stories/routes/events and participant/image permissions when available.

### Technology

Capability taxonomy (engine, electronics, display/connectivity, brakes/safety, suspension), plain-language benefit, evidence and exact applicable products.

### Community

Approved events, owner stories, rides, social permissions, moderation/expiry and related product/location. The page can launch later without blocking other verticals.

## News/articles

Headline, summary, date, author/source, body, rights-cleared media, topic, expiry/evergreen status, related products/experiences and Article metadata.

## 404 and system copy

- Specific Spanish copy for invalid filters, compare limits/incompatibility, unavailable model, no dealer/service, geolocation denied, form failure/success and 404.
- Actions use real recovery paths and never promise inventory, response time or availability without source.

## Content ownership and cadence

| Content            | Minimum owner/cadence decision                 |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Product/specs      | Client product owner; verify on launch/change  |
| Price/availability | Commercial owner; expiry/regular review        |
| Location/NAP/hours | Network owner; scheduled verification          |
| Campaign           | Marketing owner; start/end and expiry behavior |
| Experience/news    | Editorial owner; freshness/rights review       |
| Legal/forms        | Legal/business owner; versioned approval       |

Exact cadence is a client dependency; the model must support it before development.
