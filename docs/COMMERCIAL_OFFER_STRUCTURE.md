# CFMOTO Uruguay — Commercial Offer Structure

This document is an internal recommendation for preparing the formal proposal. Values are in USD, exclude taxes and exclude third-party fees unless the signed offer says otherwise.

## Pricing position

Quote in USD. The project uses imported technology services and creates a durable B2B asset; USD reduces exchange-rate ambiguity during a multi-month implementation. CFMOTO may pay the UYU equivalent at the agreed billing exchange rate if required by its process.

Published Uruguay references place ordinary institutional sites and templates from hundreds to a few thousand dollars, while custom work starts around USD 4,900 and Uruguay-based software firms commonly position bespoke engagements at USD 10,000+ with listed rates around USD 50–99/hour. Those are directional market signals, not equivalent quotes. This project sits above a conventional website because it combines custom product design, structured catalog, dynamic product pages, Compare, locations, lead UX, brand content, responsive behavior, accessibility and QA.

## Initial project assumptions

The price ranges assume:

- CFMOTO supplies approved brand files, production media and authoritative product/location data.
- One primary decision-maker consolidates feedback.
- The catalog volume and migration sample are confirmed before fixed pricing.
- One lead destination and a documented routing rule are selected.
- No e-commerce, financing engine, authenticated account, custom ERP or complex CMS is included.
- Hosting and third-party subscriptions are billed directly to CFMOTO or separately at cost.

## Initial project

The initial engagement converts the validated proposal into an official, connected and launchable CFMOTO Uruguay platform. Its statement of work should package:

- final production discovery and content templates;
- approved visual-system refinement and official asset replacement;
- catalog, product, Compare, network, lead and Experience production scope;
- agreed content/data migration;
- CRM or lead-delivery integration;
- analytics, SEO, accessibility, security and deployment configuration;
- acceptance QA, documentation, training and launch support.

Price the project by accepted scope and outcomes. Maintain an internal effort and risk model, but do not present the offer as a loose number of hours.

## Pricing recommendation

### Floor — USD 8.500–10.000

Use only when scope is reduced to productionizing the core already demonstrated:

- final brand-token replacement;
- approved Home and core catalog templates;
- limited official catalog migration;
- one basic lead delivery path;
- deployment configuration and launch QA;
- documentation and handoff.

Below USD 8.500, the project should lose defined scope rather than QA, data governance or payment protection. This floor does not include broad content production, advanced CRM routing, CMS, analytics dashboard or ongoing campaign work.

### Target — USD 14.000–18.000

Recommended range for the proposal shown in the demo:

- production refinement of the full visual system;
- three catalogs and dynamic product-detail system;
- migration of the agreed local range;
- Compare;
- Dealer/Service experience with validated data;
- Quote and Test Ride integrated with one approved commercial flow;
- Racing, Adventure, Technology and Community foundations with approved launch content;
- analytics event implementation and consent requirements as defined;
- SEO production setup, accessibility and cross-browser QA;
- deployment, redirects, documentation and team handoff.

Use a fixed number within the range only after confirming catalog size, content responsibility, CRM, legal requirements and launch governance.

### Premium — USD 22.000–30.000

Use when CFMOTO requests a broader launch program:

- extensive catalog cleanup/migration or multi-variant content;
- production coordination for images/crops and copy;
- complex CRM routing, notifications or offline conversion feedback;
- CMS integration and editorial roles;
- analytics dashboards or campaign landing system;
- additional launch content and training;
- extended warranty, launch support and reserved post-launch capacity.

Photography, video production, paid media, translation, legal work and third-party platform fees remain separate unless explicitly estimated.

## Phased commercial option

### Stage 1 — Brand, catalog and product core

Production design tokens, Home, category listings, dynamic product pages, official media/data migration, SEO foundation and deployment candidate.

### Stage 2 — Network and commercial integration

Validated Dealer/Service data, Quote/Test Ride backend, routing, privacy, abuse protection, analytics events and operational acceptance.

### Stage 3 — Brand content and growth

Approved Racing/Adventure/Technology/Community content, campaign templates, ongoing measurement and optimization.

Stages reduce commitment only when each has a usable acceptance outcome. Do not separate foundations that would create duplicate systems or temporary production hacks.

## Monthly evolution service

The contract should define a monthly capacity, release cadence, response window and treatment of unused work. It must never promise unlimited changes.

### Essential — USD 450–700/month

Included:

- dependency and security review;
- uptime/build monitoring review;
- critical defect handling;
- one planned maintenance release per month;
- small catalog or location corrections within the agreed allowance;
- monthly backup/access check and concise status note.

Optional/separate: new campaigns, substantial page work, content production, new integrations and paid-media operation.

### Growth — USD 900–1.500/month

Included:

- everything in Essential;
- scheduled catalog, price, promotion and network updates;
- up to two planned releases per month;
- SEO/content hygiene and analytics review;
- a defined batch of editorial or product updates;
- quarterly UX/performance priorities.

Optional/separate: photography, video, paid media, complex dashboards, CRM projects and major new modules.

### Digital Partner — USD 1.800–3.000/month

Included:

- everything in Growth;
- reserved delivery capacity and shorter agreed response window;
- campaign/landing support;
- continuous backlog management with Marketing and Commercial;
- deeper analytics, conversion and Core Web Vitals review;
- regular product/content releases and quarterly roadmap session.

Optional/separate: media spend, external production teams, enterprise licenses, large integrations and work beyond reserved capacity.

Final proposals should state the actual allowance or capacity behind each plan. Excess work requires written approval at a predefined rate or a separate estimate. Unused capacity does not accumulate indefinitely unless the contract explicitly allows it.

## Payment structure

Recommended for the initial project:

- **40% on signature:** reserves capacity and starts discovery/production planning.
- **35% on approved production candidate:** due when official content, core templates and agreed integrations are available for acceptance testing.
- **25% before launch/handoff:** due after acceptance criteria pass and before production credentials, final deployment or full code handoff.

For a staged contract, invoice each stage with its own start and acceptance payment. Third-party costs and commissioned media require advance funding.

## Scope protection

The signed statement of work should define:

- exact routes, modules, catalog volume and integrations;
- client-provided inputs and due dates;
- two consolidated review rounds per major design/content milestone;
- acceptance criteria and response period;
- change-request process with impact on price and schedule;
- excluded services and third-party costs;
- delays caused by missing approvals or data;
- defect warranty versus new requests;
- launch support window and monthly-service start.

Recommended defect warranty: 30 calendar days after launch for defects against accepted scope. Content changes, new devices/platform behavior and new requirements are not warranty defects.

## Monthly agreement terms

Define service hours, business days and escalation contacts rather than “always available.” A practical starting structure:

- Critical production incident: acknowledgment target within the contracted response window.
- Routine request: triage in the next agreed business window and scheduling into the next release.
- Content and campaign deadlines: require advance notice.
- Security emergency: provider may apply a narrow protective change and document it immediately.
- Major feature: separate discovery and estimate.

Do not promise a 24/7 SLA in the listed ranges.

## Ownership and licensing recommendation

- CFMOTO owns its domain, accounts, data, analytics, CRM configuration, approved copy, trademarks and supplied assets.
- After full payment, CFMOTO receives ownership or a perpetual exclusive license for project-specific code, as defined by counsel and the invoice model.
- The provider retains pre-existing tools, general components, development methods and know-how, while granting the production project the licenses needed to operate.
- Third-party open-source code remains under its original licenses.
- Portfolio, logo use and case-study publication require separate written approval.

## Hosting recommendation

Use a client-controlled static hosting account with CDN, SSL, preview deployments, rollback and environment configuration. Select the provider after confirming domain/DNS ownership, form runtime, data residency, logging and procurement. Hosting should remain transparent: platform fees, support and management appear as separate line items.

## Discount policy

Do not use a large unconditional discount to win the account. Acceptable exchanges include:

- reduced scope or slower release cadence;
- higher advance payment;
- minimum monthly commitment after launch;
- flexible launch timing;
- written permission for a case study after successful launch.

Never trade away data validation, accessibility, security, QA or payment milestones.
