# CFMOTO Uruguay — Client Input Checklist

Use the short section in the first meeting. Share the complete launch checklist only after CFMOTO confirms interest and assigns owners.

## Needed to start

### People and objectives

- One project sponsor and one person authorized to consolidate feedback.
- Contacts for Marketing, Commercial/Product, Network/Service and IT.
- Business priorities for launch and definition of the main conversion.
- Target launch window and known campaign/dealer deadlines.
- Current supplier/agency responsibilities and preferred collaboration model.

### Brand and content

- Official brand guide, logo masters, color specification and approved typography/licenses.
- Current local catalog master: models, variants, model years and publication status.
- Available official product photography/video and rights for local digital use.
- Owner of product facts and final content approval.

### Commercial operations

- Current destination for quotes and test-ride requests: CRM, email or another tool.
- Person responsible for lead routing and expected response process.
- Authoritative source for Dealer and Service locations.
- Decision on managed monthly updates versus internal editing requirement.

These inputs are enough to validate final scope and price. Do not request every technical credential in the first meeting.

## Needed before launch

### Brand assets

- Logo variants for light/dark/photo use, clear-space rules and source files.
- Approved cyan/secondary palette and typography licensing confirmation.
- Rights-cleared vehicle images with model, variant, color and market association.
- Desktop/mobile crops or permission to produce them.
- Approved launch copy for Home and Experience verticals.

### Product master data

- Stable product ID, slug, local name, category, family and use.
- Model year/variant and local publication status.
- Descriptor and factual description approved for Uruguay.
- Specification groups, units, source and verification date.
- Key facts selected for catalog and hero.
- Colors with official names and media mapping.
- Price type, currency, taxes/fees wording, effective date and owner.
- Availability/stock wording and update rule.
- Related models and Compare attributes.
- Quote, test ride and WhatsApp eligibility per model.
- Resolution of known CFORCE 450L and UFORCE 800 conflicts.

### Dealer and Service master data

- Official name and legal name if needed.
- Address, locality, department and verified coordinates.
- Confirmed capabilities: sales, service, parts and test ride.
- Public telephone and separately confirmed WhatsApp number/purpose.
- Regular and holiday hours.
- Models/categories supported and any restrictions.
- Location owner and update frequency.
- Priority validation of BIKEUP, MYM, CAFE RACE and CREDIBIKERSS spelling/data.

### Leads and CRM

- Selected CRM, form provider or email service.
- Recipients and routing by request, product, category, department/location.
- Lead owner, reassignment and escalation process.
- Expected first-response SLA and approved confirmation wording.
- Required fields and validation rules.
- Deduplication, idempotency, delivery status and manual fallback.
- Notification rules for internal teams and users.
- Test Ride eligibility, available locations and coordination process.
- Service credentials through a secure handoff process, never email or documents.

### Privacy and security

- Current privacy policy and public URL.
- Approved consent copy and versioning rule.
- Separate marketing consent if required.
- Purpose, recipients, retention, deletion/access process and privacy contact.
- Cookie/analytics consent policy.
- Approved antispam approach: honeypot, rate limit and/or challenge provider.
- Security and incident contacts.

### Analytics and reporting

- Analytics platform and account owner.
- Approved conversion/event definitions.
- Definition of valid lead versus assisted conversion.
- Campaign/UTM convention and allowlist.
- CRM/offline result fields that may be connected without exposing PII.
- Reporting audience and review cadence.

### Domain and infrastructure

- Production domain and DNS owner.
- Client-owned hosting account and billing owner.
- Access roles for repository, deployment and preview environments.
- Approved production/staging environment variables.
- Email sending domain requirements.
- Backup, rollback, monitoring and incident expectations.
- Required security headers, data residency or procurement constraints.

### SEO and migration

- Search Console and analytics ownership.
- Current URL export/sitemap and priority legacy URLs.
- Redirect approval and content retirement decisions.
- Approved titles/descriptions where commercial review is required.
- Organization/legal identity for safe structured data.

### Acceptance and launch

- Named approvers for brand, product, locations, legal, leads and technical launch.
- Content-freeze date and acceptance window.
- Lead-routing acceptance evidence.
- Browser/device list required by CFMOTO.
- Launch communication owner, rollback authority and post-launch support plan.

## Client ownership principle

CFMOTO should retain control of domain, DNS, hosting, analytics, CRM, brand files and business data. Provider access should be role-based, revocable and limited to the work agreed in the contract.
