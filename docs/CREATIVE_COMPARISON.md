## Anti-template test

### Apex

Passes because product scale, mechanical crops, physical performance data and track/editorial rhythm are structural. It fails if implemented as “dark hero + neon line + generic cards”; those choices are explicitly prohibited.

### Engineered Clarity

Passes conditionally. Real product geometry, category-specific facts and physical callouts must drive the grid. It fails if replaced by floating white panels, abstract gradients or generic dashboard metrics. This is the direction with the highest anti-template vigilance requirement.

### Open Terrain

Passes because route, terrain, machine capability and ownership form a real narrative. It fails if photography becomes interchangeable mountain imagery with inspirational captions. Product must remain large and claims/location authentic.

## Anti-copy test

- **Apex:** shares motorsport principles with many motorcycle brands, but must not copy Kawasaki/Ducati/KTM layouts, livery devices or typography. CFMOTO’s broad SR + MT + ORV portfolio and cyan-as-pulse create its own sequence.
- **Engineered Clarity:** avoids copying any one brand, but generic premium minimalism is a bigger threat. Product-specific form and verifiable engineering must remain visible.
- **Open Terrain:** must reject familiar orange/earth palettes, expedition badges and outdoor clichés. Cyan wayfinding, accessible premium tone and work/sport breadth preserve CFMOTO fit.

## Commercial test

### Apex

Creates the clearest before/after moment in a pitch and can make 450SR feel internationally competitive. Commercial credibility depends on proving that catalog, lead and network screens remain calm and buildable.

### Engineered Clarity

Best demonstrates a durable platform: easier content governance, compare, responsive behavior and expansion across categories. It may need a strong campaign prototype to convince stakeholders it is not merely a better catalog.

### Open Terrain

Offers a compelling local story and strongest emotional bridge to MT/ORV. It needs approved, high-quality regional photography; without it, the pitch loses its differentiator faster than the others.

## Recommended direction

### Direction 1 — Apex / Performance Editorial

Recommended as the creative north star, by a narrow margin despite the score total. It makes the largest brand leap, gives 450SR/Racing a memorable stage and answers the “recognizable without logo” test most convincingly. Its stronger distinctiveness is valuable in a commercial redesign where a merely competent catalog would undersell CFMOTO’s current international ambition.

The recommendation is conditional:

- prove one representative light functional flow (listing → compare → quote) before approving visual extremes;
- validate CFORCE, UFORCE and ZFORCE compositions, especially work/utility;
- require real asset/crop and performance budgets;
- cap graphic/motion devices so the system does not become racing-only;
- use the shared calm UI rules, regardless of creative direction.

If the client cannot supply campaign/product assets of sufficient quality, Direction 2 becomes the safer and more credible choice.

## Approved hybrid

The strongest controlled combination is:

- **Apex** for Home, product openings, Racing and campaign transitions;
- **Engineered Clarity** for listing, key facts, Technology explanation, specs, compare, forms and dealer/service;
- no import of Open Terrain as a third visual system; its useful photography principles can inform approved Adventure/Community content inside the chosen system.

**APPROVED 2026-09-13.** This is the official creative direction for Phase 3. It must define one type hierarchy, one color/cyan behavior, one grid and spacing system, one container model, one shape grammar, one component/state language, one icon family, one motion scale and one responsive behavior. Otherwise it becomes two sites stitched together.

## Decision outcome

Human selection completed: **Hybrid — Apex + Engineered Clarity**.

Principle: **Expressive where emotion matters. Precise where decisions matter.**

Phase 3 is authorized to translate this direction into one shared Design System. Brand book, official typography/licensing status, logo masters, cyan specification and asset rights remain dependencies before final tokens/components can be approved.
